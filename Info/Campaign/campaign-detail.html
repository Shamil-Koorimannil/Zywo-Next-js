The Crisis

The Challenge

Mouzy Avil Milk rose to fame as a viral phenomenon across Kerala, known for its signature yellow color palette and rich, layered milk dessert. However, this success triggered a massive wave of unauthorized copycat stores mimicking Mouzy's exact yellow storefronts and confusing loyal customers.

Zywo was tasked with executing an aggressive, culturally resonant campaign to help the public instantly recognize the genuine Mouzy experience from low-quality knockoffs.

Strategic Response

â€œManjayellam Mouzy Allaâ€

Playing on the famous Malayalam proverb "Not everything that glitters is gold", we created: "Manjayellam Mouzy Alla" (Not everything that's yellow is Mouzy).

The campaign celebrated Mouzy's proprietary recipe, authentic ingredients, and certified trademark while using sharp wit to poke fun at imitators trying to ride on borrowed yellow paint.

Creative Execution

Multi-Channel Rollout

We saturated major commercial highways, street junctions, store frontages, and social channels with vibrant typography posters, verified store locator tags, and fast-paced reel campaigns.

Outcome

Market Solidification

The campaign successfully stemmed brand dilution, empowered franchise partners with renewed pride, and reaffirmed Mouzy as the unquestioned gold standard in dessert drinks across Kerala and UAE outlets.
