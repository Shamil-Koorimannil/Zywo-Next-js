Who We Are

Zywo was founded on a singular conviction: Every Impression Matters. We exist to ensure that brilliant products receive the creative caliber and strategic clarity they deserve.

Origin Story

From observation to execution.

In 2024, at an Indian business conference, our founder Rayan Hany met numerous ambitious startup founders. Their products were exceptional, but their branding was largely invisible—hidden behind generic templates and disconnected advertising.

Zywo was established as an antidote to cookie-cutter marketing. Today, operating from Calicut and Malappuram, our multidisciplinary team of designers, cinematographers, engineers, and growth marketers partners with ambitious regional and national brands.

Creative Direction

Rayan Hany

Founder & Creative Director

A hands-on visualizer and brand strategist with a degree in Computer Applications and specialized training from the Evoka School of Advertising. Rayan bridges technical logic with evocative art direction.

He leads Zywo's creative output, overseeing brand identities, commercial video aesthetics, and multi-channel marketing campaigns. His standard is uncompromising: in an age of sensory overload, ordinary design is an expensive mistake.
